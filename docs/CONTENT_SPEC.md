## Title
Procedure Text — Store Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## Definition & Purpose
Gives step-by-step instructions to achieve a specific goal. Retail context includes SOP, cash handling, merchandise display rules, and return processing.

## Generic Structure
1. Goal / Aim
2. Materials / Equipment
3. Steps / Methods

## Key Language Features
- Imperative Sentences / Action Verbs
- Sequence Adverbs
- Clear & Precise Directives

## Retail Vocabulary Corner
1. Scan — Memindai barcode
2. Receipt — Struk belanja/bukti pembayaran
3. Checkout — Area/proses pembayaran kasir
4. Verify — Memeriksa/memastikan kebenaran data
5. Cash Drawer — Laci uang kasir

## Reading
**HOW TO PROCESS CUSTOMER CHECKOUT USING A POS TERMINAL**

Goal:
complete a retail sales transaction efficiently and accurately using a POS terminal.

Materials:
1. POS Terminal & Cash Drawer
2. Barcode Scanner
3. Thermal Receipt Paper
4. Customer’s Merchandise

Procedure sequence:
1. FIRST — GREET
2. NEXT — SCAN
3. THEN — VERIFY
4. AFTER THAT — ASK for PAYMENT METHOD
5. IF cash — INSERT money and ENTER amount
6. FINALLY — PRINT receipt, ATTACH it, HAND over items

Use exact source wording in final JSON.

## Formal Reading Glossary
1. Greet
2. Scan
3. Verify
4. Payment method
5. Insert
6. Attach

Important:
- Enter, Change, Hand over may occur in reading but are not formal glossary targets.
- Cash Drawer belongs to Vocabulary Corner, not formal reading glossary.
